I am Chuanbeibei Shi, a Ph.D. student at the Telecommunications Lab of Saarland University, which I joined in February 2025. My Ph.D. is funded by the Saarland/Intel Joint Program on the Future of Graphics and Media. I work on 3D computer vision for next-generation visual media within the FROST project: light-field scene representations, light-field rendering and multi-view depth estimation.

My background combines computer vision and robotics, with a focus on multi-modal 3D perception and RGB–depth sensor fusion: fusing LiDAR point clouds with camera images for object detection, RGB-D fusion for autonomous drone perching, active vision under limited fields of view, and visual-inertial state estimation for multi-robot aerial systems.

I received my M.Sc. in Robotics from the University of Bristol in 2024 and my B.Sc. in Robotics from Beijing Institute of Technology in 2022, with a Distinguished Bachelor's Thesis on LiDAR–camera active vision, supervised by Prof. Yushu Yu, Prof. Vincenzo Lippiello and Dr. Mauro Bellone.
